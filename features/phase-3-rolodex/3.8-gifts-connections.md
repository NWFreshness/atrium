# Feature 3.8: Gifts and connections

**Phase:** 3 — Rolodex
**Status:** completed
**Estimated effort:** 4–5 hours
**Depends on:** 3.3, 3.7
**Blocks:** nothing

## Goal

A person has a gift list and two-sided connections to other people in the same tenant. Gift ideas surface when a date is within 30 days.

## Scope

Include:

- Gifts on the person page: idea, given, received; name, occasion, date
- Outstanding ideas surface on the person page when an important date is within 30 days
- Connections created from either person’s page: partner, parent/child, sibling, colleague, other
- Parent/child stores `aIsParent` so the inverse reads correctly (Sam is Kate’s parent → Kate shows as Sam’s child)
- Connections clickable to the other person
- Delete person does not leave broken connections (cascade from 3.1; prove in UI/tests)
- Other person picker limited to session tenant
- Unit tests for gifts, both-sided connections, inverse labels, cascade

Exclude (for now):

- Multi-person gift registry
- Graph visualization beyond the list on the person page
